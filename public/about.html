Two ways to play

One is for friends scattered across time zones. The other is for everyone squeezed onto the same sofa.

🔗 Online

Everyone on their own screen

  1. Make a game and you get a four-character code — read it out, no link needed.
  2. Friends open the site, type the code and a name. That's the whole sign-up.
  3. Take turns drawing while everybody else races to guess. Early guesses score more than fast typing.
  4. Short of people? Add up to three bots that draw with a genuinely human wobble.
📺 Party mode

One telly, a phone each

  1. Cast one device to the TV — that's the board and the clock.
  2. Everybody joins from their own phone; the host sorts them into two to four teams.
  3. One team huddles over their phones and agrees a word. It only ever appears on their team's phones.
  4. They call someone over from the other team, show them the phone, and that person draws while their team shouts.

What's in the box

🎯

Words that don't repeat

Around 5,000 words across four difficulties, dealt off a circular deck that carries between games — so a second game tonight can't hand you the same words as the first.

🤖

Bots that draw like people

Hand-authored doodles replayed with tremor, hesitation and the occasional mistake they then undo. Nobody can tell which player is the robot.

💡

Hints you have to earn

One personal hint reveals an extra letter — only for you. Guess five words correctly and you earn another.

🖼️

A scrapbook of the night

Every drawing is kept. Swipe back through the whole evening's masterpieces with the final scores, long after the game ended.

⌨️

Your keyboard, your choice

Type on your phone's own keyboard without the board scrolling away, or use the built-in pad with hold-to-delete.

🚪

Drop out, walk back in

Lost signal doesn't end the night. The game holds open, your seat is kept, and whoever made the room gets the crown back.

Themes for this crowd

Pick one, pick five, or throw the lot in. Each pack is 100+ words, and a theme takes over the entire word bank for the night.

House rules

ThingHow it works
Players2 to 10, at least one of them human. Bots fill the gaps.
Rounds3, 5 or 10 — one full cycle through everybody is a round.
Draw time60, 75 or 120 seconds, changeable mid-game.
DifficultyEasy for kids · Medium for casual · Hard for sweaty · Ultra for no mercy.
Scoring100 for the first correct guess, then 85, 70, 60, 55, 50 — plus up to 25 for time left. The drawer earns 35 per solver.
LettersNever write the word on the board. That's the one rule.
No accounts, no ads, no app to install. Type a name and you're playing.
